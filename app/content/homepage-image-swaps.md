## Ratio notes — where the existing crop will not suit the brief

- **#1 Hero.** The slot is wide, the brief is wide — fine. Export the still at 16:9 (≥ 2400px wide) or supply the loop as MP4 ≤ 4.5 Mbps. **Code note:** `Hero.tsx` renders an `<Image>` only; a video loop needs a `video` branch (same `object-cover` fill, poster = the still). Not in this task.
- **#2 Baseline flat-lay.** Portrait slot. Shoot the bleacher flat-lay vertically and export ≈ 4:5 (e.g. 2400×3000). A landscape flat-lay will lose the ends of the kit.
- **#3 Founder poster.** The current poster is 16:9 but the card is portrait, so the existing poster is already heavily cropped. Export the new poster at ≈ 4:5 with Gabriel centred; the film itself can stay 16:9 (`object-cover` crops it the same way).
- **#5–7 Pillars.** Wide bands with fixed heights — a 2:1 export (e.g. 2400×1200) keeps the subject safe at every breakpoint. For #5 the slot is a video today; a still works too (switch `media.kind` to `image`).
- **#8 Roster.** The brief asks for a _wide_ shot of four athletes across the stands, but the slot is near-square on mobile and tall on desktop. Either export ≈ 1:1 (≥ 1400×1400) with the four athletes composed inside the central square, or accept a tighter frame (two or three athletes). The current 696px source already upscales; the new file should be ≥ 1400px on the short side.
